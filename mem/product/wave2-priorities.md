# Wave 2 priorities

## Build order (do not skip or parallel)

1. **Workspace Templates**
   - Converts abstract AI power into concrete business systems.
   - Each template ships: prompts, workflows, asset scaffolds, project structure, provider setup, roadmap, reusable deliverables.
   - Examples: AI Outreach Agency, AI Automation Agency, Faceless Content Agency, LinkedIn Ghostwriting, SEO Agency, Resume Studio, Freelance Web Dev, AI Research Ops, Creator Growth.

2. **Opportunity Engine (Hustle Finder evolution)**
   - The emotional "why HustleAI exists."
   - Skill analysis → AI leverage score → income paths → 30-day execution plan → "Open Workspace" launches prebuilt operational setup.
   - Acts as acquisition engine + onboarding engine + growth loop.

3. **Shared Project Context**
   - The quiet moat. The more connected the workspace becomes, the harder it is to leave.
   - Cross-tool memory, provider preferences, deliverable history, client context.

4. **Team / Seats**
   - Only after workspace feels truly useful for solo operators.
   - Do NOT prematurely optimize for enterprise complexity.

## What to avoid

- Do NOT become "another automation platform" (Make, Zapier, Gumloop, Relay, n8n, Lindy already exist).
- Focus instead on: operational systems for online work.
- Position as: "AI agency OS" / "freelancer operating system" / "AI-native work execution layer."

## Long-term moat: reusable operational intelligence

Over time HustleAI becomes:
"the place where online operators discover and run proven AI business systems."

This means surfacing:
- Best-performing workflows
- Best-performing prompts
- Best-performing client systems
- Best-performing agency setups

Very few companies are positioned here properly right now. This is the gap to own.

## Guardrail

Protect focus. Do not build Wave 3/4 systems until Wave 2 is complete and stable. No premature marketplaces, no enterprise features before solo-operator product-market fit.
